Moving cities made easier

From apartment search to furniture, Wi-Fi and move-in, all in one place.

May 28, 20254 min read

Daniel's job offer in Chicago came with a start date six weeks away and a problem every relocating renter knows: he was living in Philadelphia, he had never visited his new neighborhood, and he had to find a home from a laptop.

The apartment was only one of about fifteen things on his list. There was also the lease, the internet, the furniture, the insurance, and the question of who would be home when the couch arrived.

One conversation instead of fifteen

Working with USWOO, Daniel described what mattered: a short walk to the L, a quiet building, a budget that left room for the city's winters. His advisor shortlisted places, arranged video tours so he could see each one in real time, and flagged the details a listing photo does not show, like street noise and natural light.

“I signed my lease from my couch in Philadelphia and I didn't have a single regret when I walked in.”

Move-in, handled

With the lease signed, the rest was scheduled around his arrival date: Wi-Fi activated for the day he got there, furniture delivered and assembled, renter's insurance in place. When he turned the key, the apartment was already a place to live rather than a place to start working on.

What he would tell the next person

Daniel's advice for anyone moving cities is simple: start with the people, not the platform. A good search is the one where someone who knows the city is on your side, and where nothing important is left for the last week.
