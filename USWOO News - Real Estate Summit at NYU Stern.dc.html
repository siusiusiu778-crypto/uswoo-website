USWOO Sponsors NYU Stern Real Estate Club’s “Powering the Future” Real Estate Summit

Alongside JLL, Goodwin, Corcoran Sunshine and Naftali Group, USWOO joined the sponsor lineup of the Stern Real Estate Club’s annual summit.

USWOOMar 6, 20263 min read

USWOO, together with its sister brand Overseas Student Service, was a sponsor of the Powering the Future Real Estate Summit, hosted by the Stern Real Estate Club at NYU Stern on March 6.

The summit brings together students and industry professionals around the future of real estate. This year’s sponsors included:

  • JLL
  • NYU Stern Chao-Hon Chen Institute for Global Real Estate Finance
  • Goodwin
  • Corcoran Sunshine
  • Naftali Group
  • USWOO · Overseas Student Service

Why we were there

Many of NYU’s international students and young professionals start their New York journey with a housing search. Supporting the Stern Real Estate Club is one way USWOO connects with the next generation of real estate talent and with the people who will shape how the city lives and works.

At the summit, USWOO’s banner in the lobby introduced the team’s three areas of work: leasing solutions, real estate brokerage, and marketing and branding.

Looking ahead

USWOO is grateful to the Stern Real Estate Club for the invitation and to everyone who stopped by. We look forward to continuing to support the student and professional real estate community in New York and in the cities we are growing into.
