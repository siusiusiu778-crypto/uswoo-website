Find Your Place. Feel at Home.

USWOO helps international students and young professionals feel at home in a new city, with thoughtful support that goes beyond finding a place to live.

01 Our Mission

What we care about most.

Whether it's your first apartment tour or your third year in the city, this is what USWOO shows up for.

01

We Know What It’s Like

Moving to a new country comes with a lot of unknowns. Finding a home is only one part of it. We understand the questions, the small worries, and all the details that come with starting over somewhere new.

02

We Take Care of the Details

From finding the right apartment to getting settled into everyday life, we’re here to make things feel easier and less overwhelming. Because when you’re far from home, the little things matter.

03

We’re Here Beyond Move In

Our support doesn’t end when you get the keys. As you settle in, meet people, and build your life here, we want USWOO to be a familiar place you can come back to.

02 Our Story
